# Deception and Honesty

When interests diverge, a sender may gain by sending the signal that
usually means something else. If deception pays too often, the signal
stops meaning anything at all.

A rough condition for a signal to stay informative: the gain $g$ from a
lie, weighed by how often it is told, must stay below the loss $\ell$ it
causes when it is believed,

@begin(equation){#eq-honesty}
q\,g < (1 - q)\,\ell .
@end(equation)
