# Media Gallery

Real images and film in the vault, embedded with `![[...]]` — press ⌘E
to see them rendered. Everything here is **public domain**, fetched from
Wikimedia Commons; sources below.

## Ariadne, with the thread that names this app

![[Waterhouse - Ariadne.jpg]]

*Ariadne* (1898), John William Waterhouse. Asleep at Naxos — presumably
resting after all that indexing.

## The labyrinth itself

![[Burne-Jones - Theseus and the Minotaur.jpg]]

*Tile Design — Theseus and the Minotaur in the Labyrinth* (1861),
Edward Burne-Jones. Note the thread paying out behind Theseus: the
original backlink. (Play the [[Adventure]] for Clew's version.)

## Earthrise

![[NASA - Earthrise.jpg]]

*Earthrise* (1968), Bill Anders, Apollo 8 / NASA. The most famous
photograph ever taken from another world.

## Moving pictures

![[Lumiere - Arrival of a Train (1895).webm]]

*L'Arrivée d'un train en gare de La Ciotat* (1895), the Lumière
brothers — cinema, one year in. Video files get native playback
controls in reading mode and in canvas nodes.
